summary: "A standing rule: one new recipe a week, no substitutions on the first attempt."
---

The rule is simple — one recipe I've never made, once a week, exactly as
written the first time. Substitutions are allowed only on the second attempt,
once I know what the dish is supposed to taste like.

It's the same discipline as running a baseline before you tune anything, and it
has been just as useful in a kitchen.
