title: "The three-statement sanity check"
date: 2026-05-21
summary: "Before anyone reads a forecast, it has to survive four checks. None of them involve the forecast being right."
tags: [forecasting, modeling]
---

A financial model that nobody can audit is a spreadsheet-shaped opinion. Four
checks before I send one anywhere.

1. **The balance sheet balances** — in every projected period, not just the
   last. Put the check row at the top where it's impossible to miss.
2. **Cash ties out** — closing cash on the balance sheet equals ending cash from
   the cash flow statement.
3. **No hardcoded numbers inside formulas.** Assumptions live in one clearly
   marked block, in a distinct color, and nowhere else.
4. **The base case is boring.** If the base case requires everything to go
   right, it's the upside case with the wrong label.

## Sensitivities beat point estimates

One number invites argument about that number. A two-way table over the two
drivers that actually matter moves the conversation to which cell you believe,
which is the conversation worth having.
